ARTIST BIOGRAPHY

Jerry “J5” Wright

Jerry “J5” Wright is a singer, songwriter, music producer, executive producer and minister whose sound brings together inspirational music, gospel conviction and the warmth of R&B. His music is created for real life—from songs of worship and surrender to records that celebrate love, perseverance and joy.

Previously introduced through the anniversary ballad “I Can’t Imagine,” J5 returns in 2026 with a broader, energized body of work. We Make Alot of Noise, set for release on Friday, September 11, 2026, presents ten original tracks written and produced by Minister Jerry Wright, with selected co-production from Le’Quan Walsh, Young Fortune and Jisams.

With a distinctive vocal presence and a message-centered approach, J5 is available for interviews, station drops, performances and promotional appearances.
